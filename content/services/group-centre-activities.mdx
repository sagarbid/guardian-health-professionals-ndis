## What this support includes

Group and centre activities provide structured opportunities to build skills and connect with others in a supportive environment.

Activities may include:

- Social groups and community participation outings
- Skill-building sessions (communication, routines, confidence)
- Interest-based activities designed to be inclusive and welcoming

## Benefits

- Improved confidence and social connection
- New skills practised in a safe environment
- Meaningful participation and routine

## Pricing note

These supports may be **NDIS-funded** (depending on your plan).
