## What this support includes

Community Nursing Care provides nursing supports in the community when clinically appropriate and aligned to participant goals.

Supports may include (as clinically indicated):

- Medication support (as authorised and within scope)
- Wound and continence support
- Health monitoring and education
- Liaison with participants, families and relevant health professionals

## Our commitment

- Consent, privacy and dignity are central to care
- Clear documentation and communication
- Support that fits within NDIS goals and agreed plans

## Pricing note

Nursing supports may be **NDIS-funded** depending on your plan.
