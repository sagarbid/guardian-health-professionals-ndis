## What this support includes

Household Tasks support helps with everyday tasks that keep your home running smoothly.

Depending on your goals, supports may include:

- Cleaning and tidying (kitchen, bathroom, living areas)
- Laundry support and household organisation
- Meal preparation support
- Routine building so tasks feel more manageable

## Benefits

- A safer, more comfortable home environment
- Reduced overwhelm and improved daily routines
- Support that promotes independence where possible

## Pricing note

Household Tasks is often **NDIS-funded** (subject to your plan).
