## What this support includes

We help participants travel safely and confidently to the places that matter.

Supports may include:

- Assistance getting to appointments, shopping, education or work
- Support to attend social activities and community events
- Help planning routes and building travel confidence
- Transport support as agreed in your plan and roster

## Benefits

- Greater community participation and social connection
- Reduced stress around getting to and from activities
- Support to build independence over time

## Pricing note

Travel and transport supports can be **NDIS-funded** depending on your plan.
