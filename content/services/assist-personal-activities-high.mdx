## What this support includes

Assist Personal Activities (High) provides higher intensity personal care supports when that level of assistance is required and funded in the participant’s plan.

Supports are always delivered with:

- Consent and privacy
- Clear communication and respectful boundaries
- A focus on routines that support independence where possible

## Benefits

- Safe, consistent support for higher intensity needs
- Reduced stress for participants and families
- Support aligned to your goals and daily routines

## Pricing note

This support is typically **NDIS-funded** (subject to your plan and service agreement).
