## What this support includes

Life changes can be stressful — new routines, new environments, and new goals. Assist-Life Stage, Transition provides practical support to help you adapt and stay connected.

Supports may include:

- Planning and building new routines
- Support to attend appointments, education or work-related activities
- Skills and confidence-building during change
- Support to strengthen community participation

## Benefits

- A clearer plan during change
- Reduced overwhelm and more confidence
- Support aligned to your goals and preferences

## Pricing note

This support may be **NDIS-funded** depending on your plan.
