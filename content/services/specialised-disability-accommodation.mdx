## What this support includes

Specialised Disability Accommodation (SDA) support focuses on helping participants navigate and coordinate supports around their SDA needs.

Support may include:

- Understanding SDA-related goals and support needs
- Coordination and communication with relevant stakeholders (with consent)
- Supporting routines and daily living alongside SDA arrangements

## Benefits

- A clearer path to stable, appropriate supports
- Support that respects participant preferences and decision-making
- Practical coordination that reduces stress for participants and families

## Pricing note

SDA-related supports may be **NDIS-funded** depending on your plan and eligibility.
