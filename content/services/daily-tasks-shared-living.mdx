## What this support includes

Daily Tasks / Shared Living supports participants in their living environment to build routines, skills, and confidence.

Supports may include:

- Morning/evening routines and prompts
- Meal planning and shared household tasks
- Support to attend appointments and activities
- Encouraging independence through step-by-step support

## Benefits

- A calmer, more predictable daily routine
- Improved skills for living more independently
- Support for positive shared living arrangements

## Pricing note

Supports are commonly **NDIS-funded** (based on your plan and service agreement).
