## What this support includes

Development – Life Skills supports practical, everyday skills so participants can achieve goals at home and in the community.

Life skills supports may include:

- Meal planning and basic cooking skills
- Budgeting, shopping and money handling
- Personal organisation and time management
- Communication and social skills practice
- Building confidence for community participation

## Benefits

- More independence and confidence
- Skills that support long-term goals
- Support that is paced to you, not rushed
