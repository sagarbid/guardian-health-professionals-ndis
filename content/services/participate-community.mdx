## What this support includes

Participate Community supports people to engage in community life in a way that feels safe, enjoyable and meaningful.

Supports may include:

- Attending social activities, clubs and events
- Support to build confidence in community settings
- Transport and planning support where appropriate
- Encouraging independence with step-by-step support

## Benefits

- More connection and belonging
- Increased confidence outside the home
- Participation aligned to your interests and goals

## Pricing note

This support may be **NDIS-funded** depending on your plan.
