## What this support includes

Assist Personal Activities supports day-to-day personal routines in a way that feels safe, respectful, and empowering.

Depending on your plan and preferences, supports may include:

- Showering, dressing, grooming, toileting
- Mealtime support and prompts
- Transfer support (where appropriate)
- Building routines and independence with step-by-step support

## Benefits

- Maintain dignity and personal choice
- Support routines that reduce stress and improve comfort
- Increase confidence at home and in the community

## Pricing note

This support is typically **NDIS-funded** (subject to your plan and goals). Contact us to confirm suitability and availability.
