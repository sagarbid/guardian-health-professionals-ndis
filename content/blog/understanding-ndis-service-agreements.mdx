## What is a service agreement?

A service agreement is a written document that explains what supports you’ll receive, how often, and how changes are managed. It helps avoid confusion later.

## What to check before signing

- **Supports and schedule:** What’s included and how often?
- **Pricing and billing:** What funding type is used (plan-managed, self-managed, NDIA-managed)?
- **Cancellations:** How much notice is needed to avoid fees?
- **Communication:** Who do you contact if something changes?
- **Complaints:** How do you raise concerns safely and respectfully?

## Choice and control matters

You should feel comfortable asking questions. A good provider will explain things in plain language and respect your decisions.

## Tip

Ask for a copy in writing and take time to read it. If you need support to understand the agreement, you can talk to your support coordinator or advocate.
