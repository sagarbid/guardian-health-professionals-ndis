## What are “life skills” supports?

Life skills supports help you build everyday skills and routines so you can do more of what matters to you — at your pace.

## Start with your goals

Try writing 2–3 practical goals, for example:

- Cook two simple meals each week
- Build a morning routine that reduces stress
- Feel confident catching transport to a community activity

## What good support looks like

Look for supports that are:

- **Plain and practical** (simple steps, not jargon)
- **Respectful** (your preferences and consent matter)
- **Consistent** (same people and routines where possible)
- **Goal-linked** (each session connects to your plan goals)

## Questions to ask a provider

- How will you tailor supports to my routines?
- How do you track progress and share updates?
- What happens if the support worker is unavailable?
- How do you communicate with my support coordinator (with consent)?
