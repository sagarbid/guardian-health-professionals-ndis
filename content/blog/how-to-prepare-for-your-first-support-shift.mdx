## Before the first shift

It helps to write down a few key preferences so support feels comfortable from day one.

## A practical checklist

- **Your goals:** What would make today a “good day”?
- **Routines:** What’s the usual order of things (morning/evening)?
- **Communication:** Do you prefer prompts, visuals, notes, or quiet support?
- **Safety:** Any risks to be aware of at home or in the community?
- **Privacy & dignity:** What are your boundaries and consent preferences?

## On the day

Start small. A good support worker will:

- Ask before assisting
- Explain each step
- Check in regularly
- Respect your pace

## After the shift

Take 2 minutes to reflect: what worked well, and what would you change next time? Sharing this early helps the support match your preferences.
